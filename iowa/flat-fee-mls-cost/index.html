What You Pay With Flat Fee MLS in Iowa

You pay $99 once to get your home listed on statewide MLS coverage. There is no percentage of your sale price and no listing agent commission. Optional upgrades, such as ShowingTime or call forwarding, are $50 each and never required.

Other Costs Sellers Should Plan For

Selling a home always involves costs beyond marketing. Common ones include closing costs and title or escrow fees, transfer or deed taxes where they apply, prorated property taxes, any repairs you agree to, and moving costs. These exist whether or not you use an agent, so ask your title company for an estimate early.

The Buyer's Agent Question

Many sellers still offer a buyer's agent compensation to attract more showings. It's your decision and your number. See who pays the buyer's agent commission for how this works today.

Estimating What You'll Keep

A simple way to estimate your net: start with your expected sale price, subtract your remaining mortgage payoff, any buyer's agent compensation you offer, closing costs, and prep or repair costs, then subtract $99 for the listing. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a flat fee MLS listing cost in Iowa?

Our flat fee MLS listing in Iowa is $99, one time. That covers a complete listing on statewide MLS coverage that syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, and other major sites.

Do I still pay a commission when I sell in Iowa?

You don't pay us a listing commission. Whether, and how much, to offer a buyer's agent is entirely your decision and is never required.

What other costs should I plan for when selling?

Typical seller costs include closing and title or escrow fees, any transfer or deed taxes that apply in your area, prorated property taxes, and repairs or prep. Your title company or county recorder can give you exact figures for your sale.
