About FlatFeeMLS

A simple, low-cost way to put your home on the MLS without paying a listing commission.

What We Do

FlatFeeMLS helps home sellers in Minnesota, Iowa, North Dakota, and Nebraska get their property on the MLS for a single $99 flat fee, so they can skip paying a listing agent's percentage commission and still reach buyers on Zillow, Realtor.com, and other major sites.

Who Runs It
