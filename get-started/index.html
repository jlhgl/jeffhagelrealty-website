Frequently Asked Questions

What is a flat fee MLS listing?

A flat fee MLS listing gets your home listed on your local Multiple Listing Service (MLS) for a one-time flat fee, rather than paying a percentage commission to a listing agent. Your home still appears on Zillow, Realtor.com, Trulia, and all major real estate sites.

Do I still need to pay a buyer's agent commission?

You set the buyer agent commission yourself — it's entirely up to you. Offering a competitive buyer agent commission (typically 2–3%) can attract more buyers, but it's your choice. You eliminate the listing agent commission entirely.

How quickly will my home be listed?

After you submit your property details and photos, your home typically goes live on the MLS within 24–48 business hours.

Can I make changes to my listing after it goes live?

Yes. Unlimited listing and open house changes are included throughout your 12-month listing term.

Do I need to buy any upgrades?

No. The $99 package includes everything needed for your MLS listing. Optional services such as pricing analysis, contract review, and transaction coordination are available only if you want additional hands-on help.
