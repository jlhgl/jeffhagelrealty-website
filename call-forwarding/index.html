Call Forwarding for FlatFeeMLS Listings

With FlatFeeMLS, buyers and agents reach you directly — your own phone number is what's published on the listing by default. Call Forwarding is an optional upgrade for sellers who'd rather keep a personal number off public sites while still having every call routed straight to their phone.

Your Number Is on the Listing by Default

With a FlatFeeMLS listing, your own phone number goes directly on the MLS listing — the same feed that syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, and everywhere else your home shows up. There's no broker call center in the middle. When a buyer or buyer's agent calls the number on your listing, they're calling you.

When Would I Want Call Forwarding Instead?

Some sellers prefer not to put a personal cell number on a public listing. Call Forwarding is an optional $50 upgrade that swaps in a dedicated listing number that forwards straight to your phone.

  • ✓Keeps your personal number off public sites like Zillow and Realtor.com
  • ✓Calls still ring straight through to you with caller ID — no screening, no delay
  • ✓Licensed agents in the MLS still see your direct contact and showing instructions
  • ✓Can be bundled with ShowingTime for one combined upgrade

Frequently Asked Questions

Does FlatFeeMLS show my personal number on Zillow by default?

Yes. Unless you add the Call Forwarding upgrade, your own phone number is what appears on your MLS listing and everywhere it syndicates, including Zillow and Realtor.com.

What does Call Forwarding actually change?

It replaces your personal number on the public listing with a dedicated number that forwards directly to your phone, so your own cell number isn't posted publicly.

Do agents in the MLS still see my real contact info?

Yes. Licensed agents accessing the MLS directly always see your direct contact info and showing instructions, regardless of whether you add Call Forwarding.

How much does Call Forwarding cost?

Call Forwarding is a $50 upgrade, and it can be bundled with ShowingTime.
