Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information FlatFeeMLS (“we,” “us,” “our”), operating jeffhagelrealty.com, collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to jeffhagelrealty.com and the forms on it.

Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you submit a form on this site — to start a listing, request an MLS upgrade, or contact us — we collect what you enter, which may include your name, email address, phone number, property address, and any notes or property details you include.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This can include your approximate location (city-level, based on IP address), device and browser type, pages viewed, and how you arrived at the site (for example, from a Google search). We also use Google Search Console to see how the site performs in Google search results; this does not identify you individually.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, prepare and manage your MLS listing, process any optional upgrades you request, and follow up with next steps by email or phone. We use analytics data in aggregate to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site — it is not used to identify individual visitors.

How We Share Your Information

To list your property, your listing details are submitted to the applicable Multiple Listing Service (MLS) for your state, which syndicates listings to third-party real estate sites such as Zillow, Realtor.com, and others — this is a standard and necessary part of how an MLS listing works.

We use third-party service providers to operate this site and process form submissions, including Netlify (website hosting and form handling) and Google (analytics and search performance). These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visitors. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, without affecting your ability to use this site.

Data Retention

We keep form submissions and listing information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and to comply with applicable recordkeeping obligations related to real estate transactions.

Your Choices

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by reaching out through the contact or get-started form on this site.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults seeking to sell real property and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out using the form on our Get Started section.
