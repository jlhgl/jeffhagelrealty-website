How Much Will You Net Selling Your Home?

Knowing your likely net proceeds helps you set a realistic price and decide whether selling makes sense right now.

The Basic Formula

Net proceeds = sale price − mortgage payoff − closing costs − buyer's agent compensation (if you offer any) − repairs and prep − listing costs.

A Simple Example

Suppose a home sells for $300,000 with a $150,000 mortgage payoff. A traditional 2.5% listing commission would be $7,500, while a flat fee MLS listing is $99 — a $7,401 difference before any other costs. Other items, like closing costs and any buyer's agent compensation, apply either way. This is an illustration, not a quote.

Costs to Include

Title and escrow fees, any transfer or deed taxes that apply in your area, prorated property taxes, repairs you agree to, and moving expenses. Ask a local title company for an estimate.
