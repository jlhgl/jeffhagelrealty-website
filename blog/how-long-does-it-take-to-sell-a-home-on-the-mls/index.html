How Long Does It Take to Sell a Home on the MLS?

There's no single answer to how long a home takes to sell — it depends heavily on your local market, price, condition, and the time of year. But the general stages of the process are consistent, and knowing them helps you set realistic expectations.

Stage 1: Getting Listed

Once you submit your listing information and photos, a flat fee MLS listing like ours is typically live on the MLS within one to two business days, then syndicates out to Zillow, Realtor.com, and other sites shortly after.

Stage 2: Time on Market

This is the stretch between going live and accepting an offer. It varies widely by local market conditions, price, and season — homes in high-demand areas priced accurately can go under contract in days; others take weeks or months. Spring and early summer tend to see more buyer activity in much of the Midwest, though this varies year to year.

Stage 3: Under Contract to Closing

Once you accept an offer, expect roughly 30 to 45 days before closing in a typical financed sale, covering the buyer's inspection, appraisal, and mortgage underwriting. Cash sales can close faster, sometimes in one to two weeks, since there's no lender timeline to work around.

What Speeds Things Up

Accurate pricing from day one, strong photos, a flexible showing schedule, and being responsive to buyer and agent inquiries all shorten the time your home spends on market.

What Slows Things Down

Overpricing is the most common cause of a slow sale. Limited showing availability, incomplete listing information, and slow response times to buyer inquiries can also stall momentum.
