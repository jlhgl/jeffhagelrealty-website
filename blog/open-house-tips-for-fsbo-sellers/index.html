Open House Tips for FSBO Sellers

An open house is one of the best ways to get buyers into your home at the same time, and as a FSBO seller you can run one yourself with a little planning.

Set It Up Before the Day

Pick a weekend window of two to three hours, announce it in your MLS listing and online, and place signs at nearby intersections where local rules allow. Declutter and clean thoroughly — see our budget staging tips.

Make the Home Easy to Love

Turn on every light, open the blinds, set a comfortable temperature, and remove pets and personal valuables. Put out a printed sheet with the price, key features, and recent updates.

Use a Sign-In Sheet

Ask visitors for their name and contact information so you can follow up, and note whether they're working with an agent. It also gives you a record of who has been inside your home.

Stay Safe

Have a friend or family member with you, lock up valuables and prescriptions, and never let visitors wander unattended.

Follow Up

Message every visitor within a day, and ask what they liked and what held them back. That feedback is useful if you need to adjust your price. Read next: how to negotiate offers without an agent.
