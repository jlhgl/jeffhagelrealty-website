Questions to Ask Any Flat Fee MLS Company

Is the fee really all-in, or are there extra charges for photos, changes, or going live? Is the listing term long enough to cover your selling timeline? Is the company a licensed broker that submits to your local MLS? Who answers buyer and agent questions, and what are the options for showing scheduling?

Check Current Terms Directly

Prices and packages change. When you compare companies, use each company's own current pricing page rather than third-party summaries, and compare total cost, not just the headline number.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I compare between flat fee MLS companies?

Compare the total fee, what's included, how long the listing runs, whether changes are free, who handles buyer inquiries, and whether the company is a licensed broker that submits to your local MLS.

Are all flat fee MLS services the same?

No. Prices, included services, listing terms, and optional add-ons vary widely. Always confirm current pricing and terms directly on each company's website before deciding.
