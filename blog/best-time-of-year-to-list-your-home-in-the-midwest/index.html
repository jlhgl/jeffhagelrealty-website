Best Time of Year to List Your Home in the Midwest

Local conditions matter more than any calendar rule, but the seasons do shape how many buyers are out looking.

Spring

Spring is commonly the busiest stretch for buyers in the Midwest as the weather improves and families plan moves before the school year. Many sellers aim to be live by March or April.

Summer

Summer still sees plenty of activity, especially for families who want to move before school starts, though vacations can slow showings.

Fall

Fall buyers are often motivated and have deadlines. Less competition from other listings can help your home stand out.

Winter

Winter has fewer buyers, but those looking are often serious. Good lighting, a warm interior, and cleared walkways help a lot in cold-weather markets.
