How to Negotiate Offers Without an Agent

Receiving an offer is exciting, but the price is only one part of it. Taking a calm, structured approach helps you get to the best overall deal.

Look Beyond the Price

Compare financing type, down payment, contingencies, inspection and appraisal terms, closing date, and what the buyer expects you to include or repair. A slightly lower offer with fewer contingencies can be stronger than a higher one with many.

Know Your Bottom Line Before You Start

Work out your minimum acceptable net proceeds ahead of time, including any buyer's agent compensation you're offering. See who pays the buyer's agent commission.

Respond in Writing

Counter-offers should be in writing on the proper purchase agreement forms so both sides are clear on terms. Consider having a real estate attorney review the contract, especially for your first FSBO sale.

Stay Professional and Patient

Keep communication polite and quick, and don't take lowball offers personally. A counteroffer keeps the conversation going.

Handling Multiple Offers

If several buyers are interested, set a clear deadline and ask for their best and final terms in writing, then compare them side by side.
