What You're Trading Off

A traditional agent handles pricing strategy, marketing, negotiations, and paperwork on your behalf, for their commission. With flat fee MLS, you take those tasks on yourself — which is exactly why it costs a fraction of the price. Whether that trade makes sense for you depends on how comfortable you are managing showings, buyer inquiries, and negotiations directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is flat fee MLS listing legal?

Yes. Flat fee MLS listings are a standard, widely used option offered through licensed real estate brokers. Your listing appears on the same MLS, and syndicates to the same sites like Zillow and Realtor.com, as a traditionally listed home.

Do I still need to pay a commission with flat fee MLS?

You pay our one-time $99 flat fee to get listed. Whether you offer any compensation to a buyer's agent is entirely up to you — it's never required. See our page on buyer's agent commission for more detail.

What do I give up by not using a full-service agent?

You take on tasks an agent would normally handle: scheduling showings, negotiating directly with buyers or their agents, and managing paperwork through closing. Many sellers find this manageable, especially with tools like ShowingTime and call forwarding available as optional upgrades.
